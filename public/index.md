Humanward Labs is a human-centered product and experience design studio in Richmond, Virginia. We design products people find useful, usable, desirable, findable, accessible, credible and valuable.

## What we do

Three disciplines. One direction: toward people.

- **Product Design:** interface and interaction design, prototypes tested with real people, and design systems that scale.
- **Experience Design:** journey maps and service blueprints that connect app, web, email and in-person touchpoints into one experience.
- **Design Strategy:** customer research, opportunity mapping and roadmaps tied to business outcomes.

## Questions, answered

### What does Humanward Labs do?

Humanward Labs is a human-centered design studio in Richmond, Virginia. We offer product design, experience design and design strategy for companies building digital products and services.

### What is human-centered design?

Human-centered design starts with the people who will use a product. Research, prototyping and testing with real users shape every decision, so the result is useful, usable and trusted.

### How does behavioral economics shape your design?

Behavioral economics studies how people think and act in everyday life: through shortcuts, habits and emotion, not pure logic. We use it with research and testing to design products that fit the way people behave.

### How does Humanward Labs design for AI?

We design AI-powered products around the people who rely on them: clear interfaces, honest explanations of what the AI is doing, and controls people trust.
